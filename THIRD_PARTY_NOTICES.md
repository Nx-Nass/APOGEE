adapted charts with different rights. The 0BSD grant in `LICENSE` covers only
original APOGEE code and original visual symbols owned by Nx-Nass. It does not
override the terms below.

## Other scientific catalogues and references

The sources below supply astronomical facts to APOGEE. Their inclusion here is
attribution, **not** a claim that their full catalogues are 0BSD, CC0 or CC BY-SA.
No blanket permission for arbitrary relicensing of these sources is asserted.

- Pulsars: [ATNF Pulsar Catalogue v2.8.1](https://www.atnf.csiro.au/research/pulsar/psrcat/) by Manchester, Hobbs, Teoh & Hobbs (2005), via the [Stellarium Pulsars catalogue](https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/plugins/Pulsars). The compact data in APOGEE are selected and transformed from this source.
- Supernova remnants: [D. A. Green, *A Catalogue of Galactic Supernova Remnants*](https://www.mrao.cam.ac.uk/surveys/snrs/), 2024 October version. The source asks users to cite Green (2025), *Journal of Astrophysics and Astronomy* 46, 14, and the catalogue version.
- Stellar-mass black holes: [BlackCAT](https://www.sc.eso.org/~jcorral/BlackCAT/) by Corral-Santana et al. (2016), *Astronomy & Astrophysics* 587, A61; the source requests acknowledgement in derivative publications.
- Astrometric black holes and other measurements: [ESA Gaia](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia), with further references in the in-app object cards.
- Selected object distances: NASA/Hubble, NASA/APOD, Chandra, JPL, ESO and Cantat-Gaudin et al. (2020) via VizieR/CDS. Per-value source links are retained in the app.

The in-app **Legend** dialog contains additional source links and provenance
notes. Please retain those notices when redistributing the HTML file.
